# Atomic Note Agent Rules

## Purpose

`03_notes/` contains validated atomic information that has crossed the required review boundary and is eligible for reuse or absorption by structured objects.

## Rules

- An atomic note represents one independently reviewable statement, not a mixed paragraph of claims.
- Preserve source evidence, relevant context, time where available, confidence or uncertainty, and related objects.
- Proposed extractions generated under `02_processing/` do not belong here until the current workflow's validation step approves them.
- Do not merge conflicting statements into a false consensus. Preserve disagreement and provenance.
- Do not create new semantic or core object types merely because a statement is difficult to classify; follow the architecture and current issue.
- Prefer updating or linking existing validated information over creating duplicates.
- Never sever the traceability chain back to the original source.

Atomic notes are reusable information units; they are not automatically final judgments, decisions, or durable object state.
